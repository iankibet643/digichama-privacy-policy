# DigiChama Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 4 November 2026

This policy explains how the DigiChama Member Companion app ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. The app is published by Razor Informatics, Nairobi, Kenya.

The app is a read-only companion for members of chamas (savings groups) that use DigiChama. It lets you view your own chama records. Your chama's officials (for example the treasurer) record payments and manage the group in the DigiChama platform. The app does not process payments.

## Information the app uses

**Account details you enter**
- Email address and password, used only to sign you in.
- A device name, sent at sign-in so each device gets its own session token.

**Information from your DigiChama account**

After you sign in, the app retrieves the following from the DigiChama servers and shows it to you:
- Profile details: names, email address, phone number, gender, bio and profile picture.
- The chamas you belong to.
- Your chama records: contributions, shares, loans, merry-go-round rounds, welfare, goals, fines and penalties, meetings, and statements.

Your chama, not the app, creates and maintains these records.

**Information we do not collect**
- We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app.
- We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, location or SMS.
- The app only requests internet access.

## How information is stored on your device

The app keeps your sign-in session in memory while the app is open. It does not save your password on the device.

## How information is shared

- **DigiChama servers.** The app sends your sign-in details and requests to the DigiChama servers over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
- **Your chama.** Your chama's officials can see the records they manage for you on the DigiChama platform.
- **Google Fonts.** The app downloads its fonts from Google's font service. Google receives your device's IP address when this happens, as described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We do not sell your personal information.

## Data retention and deletion

Your chama keeps your records for as long as you are a member and as required for the group's financial record-keeping.

To ask for your account or personal data to be deleted, email us at the address below. We may keep records that we or your chama must keep by law.

Signing out, or closing the app, removes the session from the device.

## Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask to:
- access the personal data we hold about you,
- correct inaccurate data,
- delete your data, subject to legal record-keeping duties,
- object to how we process it.

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

## Children

The app is not intended for children under 18.
